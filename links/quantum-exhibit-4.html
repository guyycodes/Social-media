Summary of Results

  1. Mass (entanglement disruption) creates spacetime curvature.
  2. Curvature is positive near the mass (gravitational well).
  3. Curvature falls off with distance (finite-range gravity).
  4. Curvature scales with mass (equivalence principle in weak field).
  5. Zero mass produces exactly flat space.
  6. Profile matches analytical prediction from the Ryu-Takayanagi inspired distance-entanglement relation.

Conclusion

Gravity-like curvature emerges naturally from the entanglement structure of a quantum information network. No gravitational field equations were assumed — curvature arises purely from the geometry implied by entanglement.
